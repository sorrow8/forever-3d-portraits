# Forever Faces

Living 3D portraits for **World of Warcraft: Forever**. Your unit frame, your target, focus, pet, party and the NPCs you talk to show an animated 3D model instead of a flat picture.

- Player, target, focus, pet and target of target
- Party frames
- Quest, gossip, merchant and trainer windows
- The model sits inside the round frame, under the ring and the level badge
- Portrait size and a dark background, all applied at once
- Falls back to the usual picture when a unit is out of sight
- Settings: `/ff`

**Install:** unzip the `ForeverFaces` folder into `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/` and restart the game.
