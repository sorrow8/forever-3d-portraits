# Forever Faces

![3D portraits and the settings window](docs/screenshot.png)

Living 3D portraits for **World of Warcraft: Forever**. Your unit frame, your target, focus, pet, party and the NPCs you talk to show an animated 3D model instead of a flat picture.

- Player, target, focus, pet and target of target
- Party frames
- Quest, gossip, merchant, trainer, bank, auction house, stable, tabard and guild windows, and your trade partner
- Your character, inspect and dressing room windows
- The model sits inside the round frame, under the ring and the level badge
- Portrait size and a dark background, all applied at once
- Falls back to the game's own picture when a unit is out of sight, dead, offline or tapped by someone else
- Settings: `/ff`

**Install:** unzip the `ForeverFaces` folder into `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/` and `/reload` or restart the game.
